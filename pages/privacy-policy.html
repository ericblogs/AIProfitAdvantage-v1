Privacy Policy

AI Profit Advantage · Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Profit Advantage (“APEP”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) may handle information when you visit or use aiprofitadvantage.online, create an account, contact us, access learning materials, or use our services.

Important: This policy is written for the current APEP website and describes our intended privacy practices. It is not legal advice. As the platform grows, we may update this policy to reflect new services, technologies, legal requirements, or data-processing arrangements.

1. Information You Provide

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, account details, messages, course-related information, and other information you choose to submit through forms or communications.

2. Information Processed Through Website Use

When you visit a website, hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the site. Depending on the services enabled on the platform, this may include information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network data, pages requested, and security logs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve APEP services and learning experiences.
  • To create and maintain learner accounts where account functionality is enabled.
  • To respond to enquiries, support requests, and partnership communications.
  • To provide course materials, resources, and service communications.
  • To protect the website, users, and our systems from misuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • To understand and improve the quality, usability, and performance of our platform.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

APEP may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for website functionality, preferences, security, analytics, or other legitimate purposes. The technologies actually used may change as the platform develops. Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential technologies, we intend to provide appropriate controls.

5. Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party providers for hosting, authentication, communications, payments, analytics, file delivery, security, or other operational functions. These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Payments

If APEP introduces paid products, courses, subscriptions, or services, payment information may be processed by a third-party payment provider rather than stored directly by APEP. Users should review the applicable payment provider’s privacy terms before completing a transaction.

7. Educational Content and AI Tools

APEP provides education about artificial intelligence and related technologies. Information that you choose to enter into third-party AI tools, including prompts and files, may be processed according to those providers’ own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit sensitive personal information to an AI service unless you understand and accept that provider’s practices.

8. Data Retention

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate operational needs, dispute resolution, security, and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of information we hold about you. You may also have rights to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the contact details provided on the APEP website. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

11. Children

APEP is designed as an educational and professional platform. We do not knowingly request personal information from children in circumstances where doing so would be unlawful. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so we can review the matter.

12. External Links

Our website may link to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those external services. Review their policies before providing information.

13. International Users

APEP may be accessed by users in different countries. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we will seek to handle it in accordance with applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will indicate when the current version took effect. We encourage users to review this page periodically.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the official APEP contact page.
