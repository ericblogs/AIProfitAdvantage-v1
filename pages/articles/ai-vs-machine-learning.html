AI vs Machine Learning vs Generative AI: What Is the Difference?

Artificial intelligence is the broad field. Machine learning is a major approach within AI that learns patterns from data. Deep learning uses multi-layer neural networks, while generative AI focuses on producing new content or useful outputs.

Why the distinction matters

You do not need to build a model to use AI professionally. Understanding the layers helps you choose the right technology, ask better questions and avoid exaggerated claims.

Practical rule

Start with the business or learning problem, not the technology label. Then choose the simplest reliable capability that can solve it.

Takeaway

AI literacy means understanding capabilities, limitations and appropriate use—not memorising terminology.
