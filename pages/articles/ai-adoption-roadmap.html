Building an AI Adoption Roadmap

AI adoption should move from useful experiments to governed capability. Start with business objectives and workflow problems, not a shopping list of tools.

A practical sequence

Assess current capability, identify high-value use cases, pilot narrowly, measure outcomes, establish governance and then scale what works.

Measure value

Track time, quality, cost, revenue, customer experience and risk. Adoption is not success unless the workflow improves.
