AI for Meeting Notes and Follow-Up

The value of meeting transcription is not the transcript. It is turning discussion into accurate decisions, owners, deadlines and unresolved questions.

Use a structured output

Ask for decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, risks and follow-up questions as separate fields.

Keep humans accountable

Participants should review important actions before distribution. AI should assist documentation, not silently assign responsibility.
