Finding Profitable AI Business Opportunities

A promising AI opportunity starts with a painful problem, an identifiable customer and a credible way to deliver better or faster value.

Evaluate the opportunity

Ask who has the problem, how they solve it today, what the problem costs and whether your proposed solution can produce measurable improvement.

Validate before building

Interview potential customers, test a small service and seek evidence of willingness to pay. Technology should follow validated demand, not substitute for it.
