AI for Research and Information Management

Good research workflows separate discovery, source capture, analysis and synthesis. AI can accelerate each stage when the source boundary and verification standard are explicit.

Recommended flow

  1. Define the research question.
  2. Collect authoritative sources.
  3. Extract relevant evidence.
  4. Compare patterns and disagreements.
  5. Synthesise with citations and limitations.

Keep the source record separate from generated interpretation so conclusions remain auditable.
