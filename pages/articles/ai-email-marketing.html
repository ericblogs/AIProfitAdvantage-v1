AI for Email Marketing: Research, Draft, Test, Learn

AI can accelerate subject-line ideation, segmentation hypotheses, draft variations and campaign analysis. The marketer remains responsible for relevance, compliance and customer trust.

Start with the customer

Define audience, problem, offer and desired action before asking for copy. Then generate variants tied to specific hypotheses.

Use real performance data

Measure opens where appropriate, clicks, conversions, unsubscribes and downstream outcomes. Feed the learning back into the next campaign.
