From AI Skill to Digital Income

Income follows useful value. The progression is capability → proof → offer → customer → repeatable delivery.

Start narrow

Choose one skill and one customer problem. Build a small portfolio example, explain the result and test demand before expanding.

Build a system

Document your process, templates, quality checks and delivery timeline. AI can increase throughput, but trust comes from consistent outcomes.
