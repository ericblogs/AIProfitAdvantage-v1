AI Skills That Increase Professional Leverage

Durable AI capability is broader than tool familiarity. It includes problem framing, prompting, research, evaluation, workflow design, data awareness and communication.

Build evidence

Show what you can improve: a faster workflow, better report, stronger campaign, clearer analysis or useful automation. Portfolios should demonstrate outcomes, not screenshots of prompts.

Keep learning

Tools change quickly. Fundamental skills in reasoning, verification and process design remain valuable across platforms.
