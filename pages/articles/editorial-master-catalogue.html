APEP Editorial Standard

150-Topic Professional AI Editorial Master Catalogue

A controlled editorial roadmap for human-led, research-aware AI education and business content.

Editorial production rule

Every published article must earn its place through a clear reader problem, original structure, useful judgment, accurate claims, relevant examples and substantive human editing. This catalogue is a roadmap, not a claim that all 150 articles are already published.

Publishing workflow

Brief → research → outline → author draft → substantive human edit → fact check → practical review → SEO/accessibility review → final editorial approval → publication.

Quality tests

Would a knowledgeable reader learn something useful? Are claims supportable? Is the article specific rather than generic? Does it contain practical judgment? Does it avoid repetitive AI-style filler? Can a reader act on it? Is the final copy genuinely reviewed by a human?
