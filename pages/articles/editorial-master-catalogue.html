Editorial standard

What makes an APEP article professional

Every article must solve a defined reader problem, make supportable claims and provide useful judgment. The objective is not volume; it is practical intelligence a serious reader can use.

  • 01Reader purpose: a clear question, decision or work outcome.
  • 02Evidence discipline: claims checked against appropriate sources and current context.
  • 03Practical depth: examples, methods, trade-offs and implementation guidance.
  • 04Editorial craft: substantive human editing, restructuring and removal of generic filler.
  • 05Quality gate: SEO, accessibility, accuracy and final human approval before publication.

APEP principle

Capability over hype.

Tools change quickly. Professional capability lasts longer. The catalogue therefore moves from AI literacy and instruction design into workflows, commercial application, governance, leadership and measurable implementation.

Publishing status: catalogue only. An individual topic becomes a published article only after its own editorial production and review gates are complete.
