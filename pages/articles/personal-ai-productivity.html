Build Your Personal AI Productivity System

Start by mapping recurring work: research, writing, planning, communication, analysis and administration. Then identify which steps benefit from AI assistance and which require human judgment.

Build around workflows

Capture reusable prompts, source files, quality criteria and review steps together. A workflow is more valuable than an isolated clever prompt.

Measure the result

Track time saved, quality, rework and reliability. If AI creates additional verification work, the workflow may need redesign.
