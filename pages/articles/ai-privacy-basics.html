Protecting Sensitive Information When Using AI

Before placing information into an AI system, understand what the data contains, what the service is permitted to do with it and who may access the resulting output.

Classify first

Separate public, internal, confidential and highly sensitive information. Apply your organisation's policies and contractual requirements.

Minimise exposure

Use only the information necessary for the task. Remove identifiers where possible and use approved systems for sensitive workflows.
