AI-Assisted Conversion Copywriting

AI is useful for generating angles, variants, objections and revisions. The strategist still owns the customer insight, offer, proof and final persuasion.

Give the model real inputs

Provide audience language, customer objections, offer details, proof and desired action. Ask for multiple hypotheses rather than one supposedly perfect draft.

Test in the market

Use performance data and qualitative feedback to decide which messages work. AI can generate options; customers determine whether they resonate.
