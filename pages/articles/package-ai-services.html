How to Package AI Services

A strong service package answers five questions: who is it for, what problem does it solve, what is delivered, how long does delivery take and what makes the outcome credible?

Productise the repeatable

Define scope, inputs, process, deliverables, review points and exclusions. Use AI to accelerate production while keeping responsibility for quality with the provider.

Make the value visible

Use examples, case evidence, turnaround time and clear deliverables instead of vague claims about AI.
