AI Workflow Strategy: Start With the Work

The best AI strategy starts by understanding the work people already do. Map the workflow, identify friction and determine where AI can improve speed, quality or decision support.

Use a disciplined sequence

Understand the task, define the desired outcome, assess risk, pilot one improvement, measure the result and then decide whether to scale.

Keep strategy connected to outcomes

A successful AI initiative should have a clear owner, measurable benefit, quality standard and governance approach.
