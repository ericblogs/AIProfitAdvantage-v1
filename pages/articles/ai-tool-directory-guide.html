How to Choose AI Tools by Job, Not Hype

The AI tool market changes quickly. A professional directory should organise tools by the work they help accomplish and explain the trade-offs that matter.

Start with the job

Define the task, required inputs, expected output, quality standard and workflow constraints before comparing products.

Evaluate the trade-offs

Consider usefulness, accuracy, privacy, security, cost, integration, reliability, exportability and the level of human oversight required.

Adopt deliberately

Pilot tools against representative work, document the result and revisit the decision as the workflow and technology evolve.
