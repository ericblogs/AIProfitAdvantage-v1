Business Prompt Templates: Design Instructions Around Outcomes

A reusable business prompt should define the outcome, context, audience, constraints and acceptance criteria. Avoid building templates around vague commands such as “make this better.”

Planning pattern

State the business objective, relevant facts, constraints, options to consider and the format of the recommendation.

Communication pattern

Provide audience, purpose, source facts, tone and required action. Ask the model to flag unsupported claims.

Operations pattern

Define the input, transformation, decision rule, exception cases and final output.

The best template is one that produces consistent useful work while still allowing a human to review and improve it.
