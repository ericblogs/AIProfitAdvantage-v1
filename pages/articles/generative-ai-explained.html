Generative AI Explained: What It Does and Where It Fits

Generative AI creates new text, images, audio, code and other outputs from learned patterns. Its practical value comes from combining that capability with clear goals, relevant context and human review.

What it is good at

Use it for drafting, transformation, summarisation, ideation, classification, explanation and structured first-pass work. It is especially useful when the input and quality criteria can be described clearly.

Where caution matters

Generated content can be inaccurate, incomplete or confidently stated. Verify important facts, protect sensitive information and retain human responsibility for consequential decisions.

Practical exercise

Take one real task, define the outcome, supply context, request a structured draft and then evaluate the result against explicit criteria.

Key takeaway

Generative AI is most valuable as a capability inside a well-designed workflow—not as an unquestioned source of truth.
