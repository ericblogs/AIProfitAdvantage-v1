Referral terms

Important questions before you participate.

Is the 40% shown in the calculator the official APEP commission rate?

Yes. APEP has set the standard referral commission at 40% of the eligible purchase amount, subject to the programme terms.

What counts as a successful referral?

A qualifying purchase must be properly attributed to the referral and satisfy the programme's eligibility and validation rules. A click or visit alone does not create a commission.

What happens if a customer requests a refund?

Refunds, reversals and other exclusions can affect commission eligibility. Final treatment should be governed by the published Referral Programme Terms.

Can I promise someone a particular result?

No. Referrals should describe the resource accurately. Do not promise income, employment, business growth or other guaranteed outcomes on behalf of APEP.

When are commissions paid?

The commission is recorded when an eligible purchase is properly attributed and validated. The actual payout timing and method remain governed by the APEP Referral Programme Terms.
