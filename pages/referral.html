Important distinction

Academy, products and services should not be treated as one identical payout pool.

The referral programme can cover the wider APEP ecosystem, but eligibility and commission treatment may differ by offer. The 40% standard rate is currently the programme reference rate for eligible transactions; final terms determine which Academy programmes, Store products or professional services qualify.

  • Referral attribution must be valid.
  • The transaction must qualify under programme rules.
  • Refunds or reversals can affect commission status.
  • Approval and payout are administrative steps; the system does not itself transfer money.

Referral Programme terms

Clear rules for fair attribution and commission sharing.

The Referral Programme is designed to recognise useful introductions. The following operating rules explain the current programme model; APEP may update eligibility, rates, qualifying offers or programme availability when commercially necessary.

40% standard commission

The current standard rate is 40% of the eligible purchase amount. A transaction must be attributed to a valid referral and qualify under the applicable offer policy before a commission is created.

Eligible offers

Paid, published APEP Academy programmes and published paid Store products are eligible by category default unless a specific offer policy says otherwise. Free courses, unpublished or test offers and Professional Services are not automatically commissionable.

Specific offer rules

APEP may apply a specific eligibility policy, commission rate, supported currency or effective period to an individual offer. A specific policy takes precedence over its category default.

Attribution and self-referrals

Referral attribution depends on the referral link and attribution data being valid. Self-referrals, manipulated attribution, duplicate claims and other activity that conflicts with programme rules are not eligible.

Refunds and reversals

If a qualifying purchase is refunded or cancelled, its associated commission can be marked reversed. A commission that has already been paid is not treated as permanently earned merely because the payout was recorded.

Approval and payout

Commission records move through validation, approval and external payout recording. Approval does not itself transfer money. A payout reference is recorded when an administrator confirms that an external payment has been made.

Currency handling

NGN and USD commissions are tracked separately. The public calculator may show a reference conversion for illustration, but it does not guarantee a payment provider's settlement exchange rate.

Programme changes

APEP may amend the Referral Programme, including commission rates, qualifying offers, payout procedures or participation requirements. Where an offer has a specific policy, its recorded effective dates determine how that policy applies.

Referral FAQ

Clear rules make better referrals.

Can I refer people to APEP Academy?

Yes. APEP Academy is a core part of the Referral Programme alongside eligible digital products and professional services.

Is every APEP offer automatically commissionable?

No. Referral eligibility should be determined by the applicable programme terms for the offer. Do not assume that every course, service, transaction or special offer carries the same commission treatment.

What is the current standard commission rate?

The current standard programme rate is 40% of the eligible purchase amount. The actual commission remains subject to attribution, eligibility, validation, refunds, reversals, exclusions and payout terms.

Does the calculator guarantee what I will receive?

No. It is a planning tool. It illustrates the standard rate and, for USD display, uses a reference exchange rate rather than promising a particular PayPal settlement rate.

How are commissions paid?

Commission records move through validation and administrative approval before an external payout is recorded. Payout timing, method and any minimum threshold should follow the final Referral Programme terms.
