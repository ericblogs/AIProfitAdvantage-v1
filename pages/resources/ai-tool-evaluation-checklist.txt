APEP AI TOOL EVALUATION CHECKLIST

Purpose: What specific problem does the tool solve?
Fit: Does it match the workflow and user skill level?
Evidence: Is there credible evidence of useful performance?
Accuracy: How are outputs checked?
Privacy: What data is collected or retained?
Security: What access and permissions are required?
Cost: What is the total cost at expected usage?
Reliability: What happens when the service fails?
Portability: Can work be exported or migrated?
Governance: Who owns the decision and output?

DECISION: Adopt / Pilot / Monitor / Reject
