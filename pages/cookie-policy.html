Cookie Policy

AI Profit Advantage Enterprise Platform (APEP) · Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on the APEP website. It should be read together with the APEP Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Current approach: APEP intends to use only the technologies reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain and improve the website. If non-essential cookies or similar technologies are introduced, appropriate consent controls will be provided where required by applicable law.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files or similar browser technologies that can store information on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember preferences, maintain sessions and understand how a site is used.

2. How APEP May Use Cookies

  • Essential functionality and navigation.
  • Authentication and account sessions where enabled.
  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Remembering relevant preferences.
  • Understanding website performance and usage where appropriate.

3. Third-Party Technologies

Some services used by APEP may set or access cookies or similar technologies. These may include hosting, authentication, analytics, payment, embedded-content or security providers. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to review, block or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Changes

As APEP adds services or changes its technology stack, this policy may be updated. The effective date above identifies the current version.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the official APEP contact page.
