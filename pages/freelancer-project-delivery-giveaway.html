What each winner receives

  • The 10-module main guide: a practical approach to planning, estimating, scheduling and managing project work.
  • Project Delivery Workbook: guided exercises to help put the system into practice.
  • 11 templates and trackers: tools to organise tasks, timelines, capacity, progress and delivery checks.
  • AI Delivery Toolkit: 10 prompts to support planning, problem-solving, communication and quality checks.
  • 14-Day Installation Workbook: a short implementation plan for building the system into your working routine.

Who this is for

This giveaway is designed for freelancers, consultants, coaches, virtual assistants, solo operators and small service providers who manage projects or client deliverables.

How to enter

  1. Join the official APEP Facebook group if you are not already a member.
  2. Find the official Freelancer Project Delivery Giveaway post published by APEP.
  3. Comment on that post with one project-management or client-delivery challenge you would like to improve.
  4. Submit your entry during the seven-day entry period stated in the official post.

There is no purchase required. One entry per person is allowed. Only entries made on the official giveaway post during the stated entry period will count.

Giveaway rules

  • Number of winners: three.
  • Eligibility: adults aged 18 or older worldwide, where participation is lawful and digital delivery is practical.
  • Entry period: seven calendar days. The official Facebook announcement will state the exact opening and closing date, time and time zone.
  • Winner selection: three eligible entries will be selected at random after the giveaway closes.
  • Winner notification: APEP will announce the selected winners in the official Facebook group and contact them through the official APEP account. Winners must respond within 48 hours so digital delivery can be arranged; if a winner does not respond, another eligible entry may be selected.
  • Prize delivery: each winner receives one digital copy. Winners may need to provide an email address privately for delivery. Do not post personal contact details in public comments.
  • Prize limits: the prize is not transferable and has no cash alternative.
  • Fair participation: duplicate, fraudulent or automated entries may be excluded.
  • Changes or cancellation: APEP may amend, pause or cancel the giveaway if necessary, with an update in the official group.

Questions?

Check the official APEP Facebook group announcement for the final dates and entry post. The website explains the rules; the Facebook group post is the place to enter.
