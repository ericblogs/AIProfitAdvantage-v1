Resource operating principle

Use a resource to produce an outcome

Define → Assess → Document → Test → Review → Decide. The objective is not to collect more templates; it is to improve the quality of decisions and execution.

For practitioners

Use the Prompt QA and Workflow Governance resources to make AI-assisted work repeatable and reviewable.

For entrepreneurs

Use the Business Case and Due Diligence frameworks to validate value before spending heavily or creating vendor dependency.

For marketers

Use the Content Operations Brief to connect AI-assisted production to audience needs, evidence and conversion objectives.

For leaders

Use the Executive Scorecard to identify readiness gaps and establish a governed path from pilot to scale.
