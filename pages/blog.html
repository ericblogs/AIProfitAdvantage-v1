Editorial promise

Professional does not mean machine-written.

APEP will not label an article “human edited” merely because a generator produced a draft. Published pieces should undergo substantive editorial work: fact checking, source review where claims require it, restructuring, removal of generic filler, audience-specific examples, practical judgment and final human approval.

Publishing gate: topic → research → expert/author draft → substantive human edit → fact check → SEO/accessibility review → final editorial approval → publication.
