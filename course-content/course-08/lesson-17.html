Lesson 17: Building an AI Marketing Funnel

Outcome

Integrate research, offer, content, acquisition, conversion, automation, and measurement into one operating funnel.

Framework

Attract → Capture → Nurture → Convert → Deliver → Retain → Refer.

Practice

Build a complete funnel map for one offer and assign one KPI to every stage.

Deliverable

Submit funnel architecture, message map, automation points, and measurement plan.

Mastery check

Identify the highest-risk dependency in the funnel.
