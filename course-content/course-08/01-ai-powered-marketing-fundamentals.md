# Lesson 01 — AI-Powered Marketing Fundamentals

## Outcome
Understand where generative AI creates leverage across research, creation, distribution, conversion, and measurement.

## Core concepts
Marketing remains a system for identifying a valuable audience, communicating a compelling offer, creating trust, and generating measurable action. AI accelerates parts of that system; it does not replace strategy, evidence, judgment, or customer understanding.

### The AI marketing stack
1. **Research:** summarize markets, competitors, reviews, and customer language.
2. **Strategy:** generate hypotheses, segment audiences, and map journeys.
3. **Creation:** draft copy, briefs, scripts, visuals, and variants.
4. **Execution:** automate repetitive workflows and handoffs.
5. **Optimization:** analyze performance and propose experiments.

## Practical exercise
Choose one product. Map five tasks where AI can save time and two decisions that must remain human-controlled.

## Deliverable
Create a one-page AI marketing opportunity map with task, input, AI assistance, human review, and expected business outcome.

## Mastery check
Explain why faster content generation does not automatically produce better marketing.
