# Lesson 20 — Capstone: Build an AI-Powered Growth Engine

## Objective
Integrate the course into a practical, measurable growth system for a real or simulated business.

## Required components
1. Customer research and evidence brief.
2. Positioning and offer.
3. Content strategy.
4. SEO/search plan.
5. Lead-generation funnel.
6. Email nurture sequence.
7. Conversion page plan.
8. Paid-media experiment.
9. Marketing automation design.
10. AI-agent operations design.
11. Analytics and attribution plan.
12. Segmentation and personalization plan.
13. Client/service delivery or internal operating model.

## AI governance
Document every AI use case, source of truth, human approval, data boundary, quality check, and failure response.

## Evaluation rubric
**Strategy 25% | Execution 25% | Measurement 20% | AI leverage 15% | Governance 15%.**

## Final deliverable
Submit a complete growth-engine portfolio containing the strategy, assets, automation map, measurement plan, assumptions, experiment backlog, and a 30-day implementation roadmap.

## Certification standard
The project passes only when another practitioner can understand the system, reproduce the workflow, identify its assumptions, and see how success will be measured.
