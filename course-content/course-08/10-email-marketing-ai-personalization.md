# Lesson 10 — Email Marketing & AI Personalization

## Outcome
Build permission-based email journeys with useful segmentation and responsible AI assistance.

## Architecture
Use lifecycle stages such as welcome, education, conversion, onboarding, retention, and reactivation. Personalization should improve relevance, not create deceptive intimacy.

AI can classify audiences, propose subject lines, draft variants, summarize engagement, and suggest experiments. Respect consent, privacy, unsubscribe requirements, and data minimization.

## Practical exercise
Create a five-email welcome sequence for one audience and define one measurable objective per email.

## Deliverable
Produce an email journey map with triggers, segment rules, content purpose, CTA, and KPI.

## Mastery check
What is the difference between useful personalization and intrusive personalization?
