Lesson 04: Positioning, Offers & Value Propositions

Outcome

Build a differentiated offer around a specific customer, problem, promise, mechanism, and proof.

Framework

For whom → What problem → What outcome → Why this mechanism → Why trust us → Why now.

Practice

Rewrite one generic offer into three sharply differentiated propositions for three segments.

Deliverable

Create an offer brief with promise, mechanism, proof, risk reversal, and call to action.

Mastery check

State the single strongest reason a qualified buyer should choose the offer.
