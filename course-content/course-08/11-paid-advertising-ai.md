# Lesson 11 — Paid Advertising with AI

## Outcome
Design measurable paid campaigns and use AI to accelerate testing without surrendering judgment.

## Framework
Define objective, audience, offer, creative hypothesis, landing experience, budget, attribution, and stopping rules before launch.

AI can generate creative variants, summarize performance, identify anomalies, and propose experiments. Validate claims and follow platform advertising policies.

## Practical exercise
Create three creative hypotheses for one campaign and define the metric that would determine the winner.

## Deliverable
Build a paid-media test plan with audience, message, creative, budget, KPI, attribution method, and decision thresholds.

## Mastery check
Why should budget allocation follow evidence rather than the AI model's confidence?
