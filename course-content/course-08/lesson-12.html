Lesson 12: Landing Pages & Conversion Optimization

Outcome

Improve conversion by aligning message, evidence, friction, page structure, and user intent.

Framework

Promise → Proof → Mechanism → Benefits → Objections → Risk reversal → CTA.

Practice

Audit a landing page and score clarity, relevance, trust, friction, and CTA strength from 1–5.

Deliverable

Produce a prioritized conversion test backlog.

Mastery check

Separate a testable hypothesis from a subjective design preference.
