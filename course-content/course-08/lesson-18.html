Lesson 18: Creating a Complete Campaign

Outcome

Translate strategy into a launch-ready integrated campaign with creative, channels, budget, tracking, and optimization rules.

Framework

Brief → Audience → Offer → Creative → Channel mix → Tracking → Launch → Optimize.

Practice

Develop a campaign brief with objectives, audience, assets, timeline, budget assumptions, and KPI targets.

Deliverable

Submit a complete campaign plan and launch checklist.

Mastery check

State the evidence required before scaling campaign spend.
