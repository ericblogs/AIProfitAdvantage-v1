# Lesson 02 — Market Research & Customer Intelligence

## Outcome
Build an evidence-led research process using customer language and AI-assisted synthesis.

## Framework
Collect primary evidence first: interviews, surveys, reviews, support tickets, search queries, comments, and sales objections. Use AI to classify and summarize evidence, not invent it.

### Research workflow
**Collect → clean → cluster → quantify → interpret → validate → act.**

Ask AI to identify recurring pains, desired outcomes, objections, alternatives, phrases, and buying triggers. Preserve source links and distinguish observed evidence from inference.

## Practical exercise
Gather 20 authentic customer comments for a product. Ask an AI model to cluster them into five themes, then manually verify every cluster.

## Deliverable
Create a customer-intelligence brief containing top pains, desired outcomes, objections, competitor alternatives, customer vocabulary, and three testable marketing hypotheses.

## Mastery check
Which conclusions are directly evidenced, and which are hypotheses requiring validation?
