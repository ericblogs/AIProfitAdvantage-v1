# Lesson 06 — Content Strategy & Content Systems

## Outcome
Design a repeatable content system tied to business objectives rather than random posting.

## Content architecture
Define pillars, audiences, stages of awareness, formats, channels, cadence, owners, and conversion goals. Build a source-of-truth content brief so one strong idea can become multiple useful assets.

AI can repurpose approved source material into drafts, outlines, hooks, summaries, and variants. Human review protects accuracy, voice, originality, and brand standards.

## Practical exercise
Take one customer insight and turn it into a long-form article, short post, email angle, video brief, and CTA.

## Deliverable
Create a 30-day content calendar mapped to funnel stage and measurable objective.

## Mastery check
How do you distinguish content volume from content-system effectiveness?
