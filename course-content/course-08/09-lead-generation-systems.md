# Lesson 09 — Lead Generation Systems

## Outcome
Design a lead-generation system that exchanges relevant value for qualified attention.

## System
**Traffic source → offer → landing page → capture → qualification → nurture → sales action → measurement.**

Define lead quality before optimizing lead volume. Qualification can use firmographics, intent, need, budget, authority, timing, or behavior depending on the offer.

AI can assist with lead magnets, qualification questions, segmentation, nurture drafts, and lead summaries while humans retain responsibility for high-impact decisions.

## Practical exercise
Design one lead magnet and its five-step journey from first click to sales conversation.

## Deliverable
Create a lead-generation funnel map with source, cost assumption, conversion events, qualification rules, and KPIs.

## Mastery check
Why can a campaign with a low cost per lead still be commercially poor?
