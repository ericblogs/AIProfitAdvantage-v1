# Lesson 08 — Social Media Marketing with AI

## Outcome
Create a channel strategy and production workflow that preserves authenticity and measurable goals.

## Framework
Choose platforms based on audience behavior, content fit, economics, and available capacity. Define objectives before formats: awareness, engagement, leads, sales, retention, or community.

AI can assist with content calendars, variations, comment classification, trend research, and repurposing. Do not automate sensitive interactions or impersonate people.

## Practical exercise
Build a channel matrix for three platforms with audience, objective, format, cadence, CTA, and KPI.

## Deliverable
Create a two-week social campaign with approved source content and a response/escalation policy.

## Mastery check
What evidence would justify dropping a channel rather than simply posting more?
