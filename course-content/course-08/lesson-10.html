Lesson 10: Email Marketing & AI Personalization

Outcome

Create permission-based email sequences using segmentation, relevance, testing, and measurable calls to action.

Framework

Segment → Trigger → Context → Message → CTA → Test → Learn.

Practice

Design a five-email nurture sequence for one audience segment.

Deliverable

Submit subject lines, email objectives, CTAs, and test variables.

Mastery check

Explain why personalization should improve relevance without becoming invasive.
