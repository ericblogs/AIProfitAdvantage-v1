Lesson 03: Buyer Psychology & Customer Avatars

Outcome

Translate customer evidence into useful buyer segments and ethical messaging angles.

Framework

Situation → Friction → Motivation → Risk → Desired transformation → Decision criteria.

Practice

Create two evidence-backed buyer avatars for one offer and list the strongest objection for each.

Deliverable

Submit two one-page buyer profiles and matching message angles.

Mastery check

Explain why an avatar must represent evidence rather than stereotypes.
