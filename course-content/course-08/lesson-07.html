Lesson 07: SEO & AI-Assisted Search

Outcome

Build search-focused content using intent, topical coverage, useful structure, and human editorial review.

Framework

Intent → Topic → Evidence → Structure → Internal links → Technical quality → Measurement.

Practice

Map ten queries to informational, commercial, or transactional intent and design a content cluster.

Deliverable

Create an SEO brief for one pillar article and three supporting pages.

Mastery check

Explain why keyword volume alone is insufficient for prioritization.
