Lesson 01: AI-Powered Marketing Fundamentals

Outcome

Understand how generative AI changes research, creation, personalization, optimization, and marketing operations.

Core framework

Strategy → Research → Offer → Message → Channel → Conversion → Measurement. AI accelerates each stage; it does not replace judgment.

Practice

Choose one product and map five marketing tasks AI can accelerate and two decisions that must remain human-led.

Deliverable

Create a one-page AI marketing opportunity map.

Mastery check

Explain where AI creates leverage and where inaccurate outputs could damage customer trust.
