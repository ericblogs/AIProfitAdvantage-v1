# Lesson 17 — Building an AI Marketing Funnel

## Outcome
Assemble research, content, acquisition, conversion, automation, and measurement into one coherent funnel.

## Funnel architecture
**Audience → attention → education → consideration → conversion → onboarding → retention → referral.**

Map every stage to an owner, asset, system, KPI, and failure mode. AI should reduce production and analysis friction while preserving approvals and traceability.

## Practical exercise
Design an end-to-end funnel for one APEP-style digital course, including traffic source, lead magnet, nurture, sales page, checkout, onboarding, and retention.

## Deliverable
Produce a funnel blueprint with assumptions, economics, assets, automations, KPIs, and experiment backlog.

## Mastery check
Where is the highest-risk handoff in your funnel, and how will you monitor it?
