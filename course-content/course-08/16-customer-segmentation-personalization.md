# Lesson 16 — Customer Segmentation & Personalization

## Outcome
Use meaningful behavioral or business differences to make marketing more relevant.

## Segmentation
Useful segments have a measurable distinction and a different action: lifecycle stage, need, product usage, value, geography, industry, or intent. Avoid unnecessary collection of sensitive data.

AI can help identify patterns and draft segment-specific messages. Validate segment stability and monitor disparate outcomes where relevant.

## Practical exercise
Create three segments for one product and assign a different offer, message, and channel to each.

## Deliverable
Build a segmentation matrix with definition, evidence, size, value hypothesis, message, channel, and KPI.

## Mastery check
What makes a segment operationally useful rather than merely descriptive?
