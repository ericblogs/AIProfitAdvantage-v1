# Course 08 Content Quality Standard

Every production lesson must contain:

1. Clear learning outcome.
2. Conceptual explanation with relevant terminology.
3. Practical workflow or framework.
4. Hands-on exercise.
5. Concrete learner deliverable.
6. Mastery check or assessment prompt.
7. Responsible-AI guidance where AI is involved.
8. No fabricated claims, testimonials, statistics, credentials, or job guarantees.

The capstone must demonstrate integration of strategy, execution, analytics, automation, and governance.
