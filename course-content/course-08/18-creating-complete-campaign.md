# Lesson 18 — Creating a Complete Campaign

## Outcome
Turn a strategic brief into an executable, measurable campaign.

## Campaign plan
Define objective, audience, offer, proposition, creative concept, channels, budget, timeline, owners, tracking, approval gates, and contingency actions.

Use AI to generate variants and production checklists, then validate every customer-facing claim and asset.

## Practical exercise
Create a seven-day launch campaign for a digital product with three acquisition assets, two nurture emails, one landing page, and a measurement plan.

## Deliverable
Submit a campaign brief, asset list, calendar, tracking plan, QA checklist, and post-campaign review template.

## Mastery check
Which campaign assumption would most damage the plan if it proved false?
