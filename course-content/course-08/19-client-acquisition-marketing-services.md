# Lesson 19 — Client Acquisition & Marketing Services

## Outcome
Package AI-enabled marketing capabilities into credible services without overpromising.

## Service model
Choose a niche, define a concrete problem, package deliverables, establish scope, create proof, set pricing, and build a repeatable prospecting process.

AI can support research, audits, proposals, reporting, and delivery. Client data and confidential material require explicit authorization and appropriate controls.

## Practical exercise
Create one productized service with scope, exclusions, timeline, deliverables, success measures, price hypothesis, and client onboarding checklist.

## Deliverable
Build a one-page service offer and a 10-prospect outreach research plan.

## Mastery check
Why is a narrow outcome usually easier to sell and deliver than a broad “full-service” promise?
