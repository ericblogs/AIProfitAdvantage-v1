Lesson 05: AI-Powered Copywriting

Outcome

Use AI to develop research-grounded headlines, leads, offers, emails, ads, and calls to action.

Framework

Brief → Evidence → Angle → Draft → Critique → Revision → Compliance check.

Practice

Create three headlines and one sales lead from the Course 08 offer brief, then critique each against audience, promise, proof, and clarity.

Deliverable

Build a reusable copywriting prompt brief and revised landing-page opening.

Mastery check

Explain why AI-generated copy must be edited against verified customer evidence.
