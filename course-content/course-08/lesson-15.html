Lesson 15: Marketing Analytics & Attribution

Outcome

Connect marketing activity to measurable outcomes using consistent definitions, attribution logic, and decision rules.

Framework

Objective → Event → Source → Cost → Conversion → Attribution → ROI → Decision.

Practice

Define a measurement plan for a funnel with acquisition, lead, qualified lead, and customer events.

Deliverable

Create a KPI dictionary and attribution assumptions.

Mastery check

Explain the difference between correlation, attribution, and causation.
