Lesson 11: Paid Advertising with AI

Outcome

Plan paid campaigns around audience, offer, creative, landing experience, budget, and measurable outcomes.

Framework

Objective → Audience → Offer → Creative → Landing page → Budget → Experiment → Optimize.

Practice

Create two campaign hypotheses with distinct audiences and creative angles.

Deliverable

Produce a campaign brief with KPI definitions and stop/scale rules.

Mastery check

Explain why an attractive click-through rate can still produce an unprofitable campaign.
