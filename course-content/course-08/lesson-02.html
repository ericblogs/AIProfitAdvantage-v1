Lesson 02: Market Research & Customer Intelligence

Outcome

Build an evidence-based customer profile from market signals, reviews, interviews, search behavior, and competitor observations.

Framework

Problem → Audience → Trigger → Desired outcome → Objection → Proof. Use AI to organize evidence, not invent evidence.

Practice

Collect ten real customer statements and classify each by pain, desired outcome, objection, or buying trigger.

Deliverable

Produce a customer-intelligence brief with evidence sources.

Mastery check

Identify which conclusions are observed facts and which are hypotheses.
