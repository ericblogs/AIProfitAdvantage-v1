Lesson 08: Social Media Marketing with AI

Outcome

Plan platform-appropriate social content, community engagement, and measurement with AI assistance.

Framework

Audience → Platform → Format → Hook → Value → Conversation → CTA → KPI.

Practice

Design one week of content for two platforms and adapt the same idea without copying the format.

Deliverable

Submit a platform content matrix and moderation rules.

Mastery check

Identify one metric that indicates attention and one that indicates business impact.
