# Lesson 12 — Landing Pages & Conversion Optimization

## Outcome
Build landing experiences that make the next action clear and measurable.

## Conversion model
**Message match + relevance + clarity + proof + friction reduction + credible CTA.**

Map the page to the visitor's awareness level. Use one primary conversion goal and support the claim with evidence. AI can audit structure, generate variants, and simulate objections; analytics determine what actually works.

## Practical exercise
Audit a landing page for headline clarity, offer relevance, proof, objections, friction, CTA visibility, mobile usability, and trust.

## Deliverable
Create a conversion-optimization backlog ranked by expected impact and implementation effort.

## Mastery check
What makes a conversion hypothesis testable rather than merely an opinion?
