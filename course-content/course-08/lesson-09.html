Lesson 09: Lead Generation Systems

Outcome

Design an ethical lead-generation system from traffic source through qualification and follow-up.

Framework

Traffic → Lead magnet → Landing page → Capture → Qualification → Nurture → Sales action.

Practice

Build a lead funnel for one target segment and define the conversion event at each stage.

Deliverable

Submit funnel map, lead magnet concept, and qualification criteria.

Mastery check

Identify the largest measurable bottleneck in your funnel.
