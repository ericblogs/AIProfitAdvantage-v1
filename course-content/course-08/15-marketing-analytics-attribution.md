# Lesson 15 — Marketing Analytics & Attribution

## Outcome
Connect marketing activity to measurable outcomes and make decisions from reliable metrics.

## Measurement stack
Define events, source systems, identity rules, attribution windows, conversion definitions, and reporting cadence. Distinguish leading indicators from business outcomes.

Use AI to summarize reports and investigate patterns, but verify calculations against source data. Avoid presenting correlation as causation.

## Practical exercise
Build a funnel from impression to revenue and identify the metric and denominator at every stage.

## Deliverable
Create a measurement plan covering KPI definitions, data sources, attribution model, dashboard views, and decision rules.

## Mastery check
Why can two attribution models produce different answers from the same campaign data?
