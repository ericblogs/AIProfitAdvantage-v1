Lesson 19: Client Acquisition & Marketing Services

Outcome

Package AI-enabled marketing capabilities into ethical, measurable services that solve defined client problems.

Framework

Niche → Problem → Offer → Proof → Prospecting → Discovery → Proposal → Delivery → Retention.

Practice

Create one productized service with scope, deliverables, exclusions, evidence, and a simple discovery process.

Deliverable

Submit a one-page service offer and prospect qualification checklist.

Mastery check

Explain how scope control protects both client value and delivery quality.
