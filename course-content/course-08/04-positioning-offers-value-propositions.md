# Lesson 04 — Positioning, Offers & Value Propositions

## Outcome
Construct a clear offer that connects a specific audience and problem to a credible outcome.

## Framework
**Audience + painful problem + differentiated mechanism + valuable outcome + proof + risk reduction.**

Positioning is the space you deliberately occupy in the buyer's mind. An offer is the complete exchange: promise, deliverables, price, proof, terms, and risk reversal.

Use AI to generate alternatives, identify ambiguity, stress-test claims, and compare message clarity. Never use AI to fabricate testimonials, guarantees, statistics, or credentials.

## Practical exercise
Rewrite a vague offer three ways for three segments. Score each on specificity, relevance, credibility, and differentiation.

## Deliverable
Produce an offer brief with ICP, problem, promise, mechanism, deliverables, proof plan, objections, price, and call to action.

## Mastery check
Why can a strong product still fail when its positioning is unclear?
