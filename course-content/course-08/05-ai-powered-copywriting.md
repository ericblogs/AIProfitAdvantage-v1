# Lesson 05 — AI-Powered Copywriting

## Outcome
Use AI as a drafting and optimization partner while retaining strategic and editorial control.

## Copy framework
Start with **audience, awareness, promise, proof, mechanism, objections, offer, CTA**. Then choose the appropriate structure: problem-solution, PAS, AIDA, case-led, comparison, or educational narrative.

AI works best when given context, constraints, evidence, examples, and a clear evaluation rubric. Iterate rather than asking for one perfect draft.

## Practical exercise
Create three headlines for the same offer, then score them for specificity, curiosity, benefit clarity, credibility, and audience fit.

## Deliverable
Produce a landing-page copy brief and first draft with a fact-check checklist.

## Mastery check
What information must an AI model receive before its copy can be strategically useful?
