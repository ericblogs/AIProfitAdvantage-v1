# Lesson 03 — Buyer Psychology & Customer Avatars

## Outcome
Translate research into useful buyer segments without relying on stereotypes.

## Core concepts
A useful avatar describes a buying situation: problem severity, desired outcome, constraints, awareness, objections, alternatives, and decision criteria. Psychology is about understanding motivation and friction, not manipulating people.

### Buyer map
**Situation → Problem → Consequence → Desired future → Proof required → Objection → Action.**

Use AI to produce segment hypotheses, interview questions, objection matrices, and message variants. Validate them against real evidence.

## Practical exercise
Create two avatars for the same offer: one problem-aware and one solution-aware. Write a different opening message for each.

## Deliverable
Build a two-segment buyer matrix with evidence, pains, goals, objections, proof, channels, and preferred message angle.

## Mastery check
What evidence would make you retire or revise an avatar?
