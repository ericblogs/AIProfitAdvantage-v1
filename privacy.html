Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AI Profit Advantage ("AI Profit Advantage," "APEP Academy," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect through our website, learning platform, courses, resources, communications and related services, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Depending on how you interact with APEP Academy, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, login credentials and related profile information.

  • Transaction information: information necessary to process purchases, enrollments or other paid services. Payment card details may be processed directly by payment providers rather than stored by us.

  • Cookies and similar technologies: information used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, understand website usage and improve the experience, where such technologies are enabled.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • provide and operate our website, courses and learning services;
  • create and manage learner accounts;
  • track course progress and provide learning support;
  • process purchases, registrations and related transactions;
  • respond to questions, requests and support enquiries;
  • send requested educational, service or administrative communications;
  • improve our curriculum, website, services and user experience;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse and protect our systems; and

We may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functions, preferences, security, analytics and service improvement. Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate notice and choices regarding non-essential cookies.
