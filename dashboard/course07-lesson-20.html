COURSE 07 • DATA ANALYTICS WITH GENERATIVE AI

20. Capstone — Build a Job-Ready Data Analytics Portfolio

Integrate the full workflow: business question, data-quality audit, Excel or SQL analysis, Power BI or Tableau visualization, Generative AI assistance, executive story, and career-ready documentation.

Acceptance criteria

  • Documented data source and assumptions.
  • Reproducible analytical steps.
  • Validated calculations.
  • Decision-focused dashboard.
  • Five-minute presentation narrative.
  • Portfolio-ready README and LinkedIn/CV project entry.

Do not claim results that cannot be reproduced from the underlying data.
