🎓 APEP Academy Certification Pathway

APEP Academy is building a practical certification pathway around demonstrated learning, applied AI skills, and responsible use of AI.

What certification will emphasize

  • Understanding core AI concepts.
  • Applying AI tools to practical tasks.
  • Prompting and workflow design.
  • Responsible evaluation of AI outputs.
  • Practical project application.

Current status

The certification pathway is being developed alongside the AI Foundations curriculum. Learners should use the course lessons and knowledge checks as preparation while the formal assessment and certificate issuance process is finalized.
