Lesson 02: Data Analytics Mindset & Career Pathways

Outcome

Develop an evidence-first mindset and understand analyst career pathways.

Framework

Curiosity + skepticism + business context + reproducibility = professional analysis.

Practice

Compare a reporting task, diagnostic task and predictive task. Identify the business decision each supports.

Deliverable

Create a personal analyst skills map covering tools, statistics, communication and AI.

Mastery check

Explain why tool knowledge alone does not make someone a data analyst.
