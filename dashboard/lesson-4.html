Lesson 4 — Neural Networks

Understand the basic architecture of neural networks and why layered representations became so important to modern AI.

Learning Objectives

  • ✅ Explain the basic idea of an artificial neural network.
  • ✅ Identify neurons, layers, weights, biases, and activations.
  • ✅ Understand how a network learns through training and error reduction.
  • ✅ Explain why deep networks can learn complex representations.
  • ✅ Connect neural networks to practical AI applications.

🧠 What Is a Neural Network?

An artificial neural network is a machine-learning model made from interconnected computational units arranged in layers. It is inspired loosely by biological nervous systems, but it is a mathematical model rather than a literal copy of the human brain.

🔢 Neurons, Weights, and Biases

A simplified neuron receives numerical inputs, multiplies them by learned weights, adds a bias, and passes the result through an activation function.

The weights determine how strongly different inputs influence the result. During training, the model adjusts these parameters so its predictions become more useful.

🏗️ Layers

Neural networks commonly contain an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer. The input layer receives information, hidden layers transform representations, and the output layer produces the final prediction or classification.

A network with many hidden layers is often described as a deep neural network.

⚡ Activation Functions

Activation functions introduce non-linearity into the network. Without non-linear transformations, stacking many simple linear operations would still behave like one linear operation.

Common activation functions include ReLU, sigmoid, and softmax, with different functions serving different roles.

📚 How Neural Networks Learn

During training, a network makes predictions and compares them with desired outcomes using a loss function. An optimization process then adjusts the model parameters to reduce the loss.

Backpropagation calculates how changes in the parameters affect the loss, while optimization methods such as gradient descent use that information to update the parameters.

🖼️ From Pixels to Patterns

In image tasks, early layers may learn simple patterns such as edges or textures, while deeper layers can combine those representations into more complex structures. This layered representation is one reason neural networks became powerful for computer vision.

💬 Neural Networks and Language

Neural networks also transformed natural-language processing. Modern language models use large neural architectures to learn statistical relationships among tokens and generate or transform language.

Transformer architectures later became especially important because they can model relationships among elements of a sequence efficiently and at large scale.

⚠️ Neural Networks Have Limitations

A neural network can produce impressive results while still making confident mistakes. Performance depends on training data, model design, evaluation, deployment conditions, and the quality of the task definition.

For responsible use, human oversight and appropriate evaluation remain essential.

💼 Why Neural Networks Matter for Business

Understanding the basic architecture helps professionals evaluate AI tools more intelligently. It explains why modern systems can recognize patterns, process language, generate content, and automate complex tasks while also reminding us that these systems are statistical models rather than human minds.

📝 Lesson 4 Summary

Neural networks are layered machine-learning models that learn parameter values from data. Their power comes from combining many transformations to learn increasingly useful representations.

Modern deep learning and language systems build on these ideas, but their impressive performance does not eliminate the need for evaluation, human judgment, and responsible deployment.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • ✅ Neural networks are mathematical models composed of interconnected units and layers.
  • ✅ Weights and biases are learned parameters.
  • ✅ Activation functions introduce useful non-linearity.
  • ✅ Training uses loss, backpropagation, and optimization to improve predictions.
  • ✅ Deep neural networks can learn increasingly complex representations.
  • ✅ Neural networks power major areas of modern AI but still require evaluation and oversight.
