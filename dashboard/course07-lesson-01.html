COURSE 07 • DATA ANALYTICS WITH GENERATIVE AI

01. Introduction to Data Analytics with Generative AI

Learn how analysts combine reliable data practices with Generative AI to move from raw information to useful decisions.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the analytics lifecycle.
  • Distinguish evidence from AI-generated suggestions.
  • Define a practical analytics question.

Practice

Choose a business problem, identify the data needed, and ask ChatGPT to propose—but not invent—the analysis plan.
