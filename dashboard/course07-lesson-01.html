Lesson 01: Introduction to Data Analytics with Generative AI

Outcome

Understand the analyst workflow and how generative AI can accelerate research, analysis and communication.

Framework

Question → Data → Quality → Analysis → Insight → Decision → Communication.

Practice

Choose a business question and identify the data required, likely quality risks and the decision the analysis should support.

Deliverable

Create a one-page analytics problem statement.

Mastery check

Explain why a good analyst starts with a decision rather than a dashboard.
