Governance standard: APEP does not infer certification from a single progress percentage. The authoritative eligibility rule requires an active enrollment plus completion evidence for every published lesson in the published course. Administrative issuance creates a unique certificate number, verification credential and auditable ledger record. Reissue supersedes the prior credential; revocation requires a recorded reason.
